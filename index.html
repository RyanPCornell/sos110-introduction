Example: How do you get the most fish? Sustainable vs Un-sustainable

10people share the lake
1,000fish in it today
+20%replenish each year
Your choice 25 fish per person, per year
over 20 years, each person gets 0 fish
0 fish left in the lake after 20 years
A second choice 12 fish per person, per year
over 20 years, each person gets 0 fish
0 fish left in the lake after 20 years

The lake refills by 20% of whatever is left — so a take of about 16 fish each can go on for ever. Above that, every year starts poorer than the last.

What is Harmonize?

Harmonize is a social media platform designed for higher education. Harmonize integrates with Canvas, allowing students and instructors to interact and easily share articles, documents, website or video links, short recorded responses, and drawings that relate to their course content. Students can also record themselves giving a presentation while screen sharing their slide deck.

Assignments · Review Assignments

Review Assignments:

  • Three Exercises, each worth 2% of your overall grade (6% total).
  • During Modules 2, 4, 7.
  • Run by TA(s).
  • Focus: group work and systems.
Example:

Review Exercise 1 — Ecosystem Services and Sustainability

In-class Zoom Whiteboard · Miro · Canva · Google Slides · InsightMaker

Objective: Collaboratively analyze the ecosystem services of Grand Canyon National Park (GCNP) and evaluate how human-driven changes impact its long-term sustainability.

Final deliverable: a single Digital Mind Map with three distinct, labeled sections.

1Native Biodiversity List
Using only official sources (nps.gov, usgs.gov): 3+ representative plant communities (Ponderosa pine, desert scrub) and 3+ native animal species (California condor, Kaibab squirrel).
2Ecosystem Services Mapping
A visual grid or web classifying the park’s benefits: one GCNP example for each of the four ecosystem service categories, with arrows linking each service to a concrete sustainability outcome — e.g. Provisioning: freshwater springs → supports desert megafauna survival.
